# Protocol of arXiv 2610.31820 ("Free Lunch for LLMs Confidence: The RL Critic Already Knows")

Wave 6, task A2. Run date 2026-11-10. Text basis: FULL TEXT, arXiv v1 (2026-10-22), Wu and Ji, saved at `../w5/parts/ft-A4b/raw/ft/2610.31820.txt`. The sub-agent extraction with every quote and line number is `../../../docs/s6/parts/proto-A2/PROTOCOL.md`; `txt:N` below refers to the saved text.

## Answer to the design question: which generations was the trained readout scored on?

**Not stated.** The paper never says whether the R-Tuning score in Table 3 was computed on the RL policy's frozen generations, the ones the probe reads, or on the fine-tuned model's own new outputs.

**Two readings are consistent with the text. The question stays unresolved** (revised after A6 F5).

- **Reading A: the fine-tuned model's own new outputs.**
  - Table 2 gives R-Tuning its own task accuracy, separate from the Original Policy rows (`txt:252-269`, `txt:303-320`).
  - Sec 4.2: "fine-tuning the policy on the confidence objective lowers accuracy on 15 of the 16 model-domain-split combinations, by 3.9 points on average" (`txt:1112`).
  - "Every prompt is answered with several samples at the RL temperature" (`txt:1104`). This does not name the answering model.
- **Reading B: a teacher-forced readout on the RL policy's frozen samples.**
  - R-Tuning appends a statement to a response and reads the probability of the confidence token (`txt:110-112`). That mechanism can be applied to any fixed response.
  - In Sec 2, R-Tuned external estimators score the target policy's fixed responses "as text" (`txt:113-115`). That is a trained readout on frozen generations.
  - Table 2 shows that the fine-tuned model generated outputs for the accuracy rows, but not which responses the Table 3 confidence was read on.

**Consequence for the design.** The reproduction (`docs/s6/PIPELINE_DESIGN_R1.md` section 2, written in Part B of this wave) runs the 2610.31820-style readout under both readings: on its own fresh generations (A) and teacher-forced on the frozen generations (B). The difference between A and B is the generation-shift term (G) for that readout, so the budget measures what the paper leaves unstated. 2609.33290 has the same structure and states it: its trained student is scored on its own samples, and its probe on the original model's (`docs/s6/parts/occ-A1a/raw/ft/2609.33290.txt:571-575, 3448`). The paper does not state the probe's generations either. The inference is the RL policy's samples (`txt:1097`, `txt:1104`).

## What the reproduction inside our framework must and cannot match

The design reproduces the configuration class: a trained categorical own-readout (append "I am sure" or "I am unsure", score the confidence-token probability), fit on the same labeled responses as a last-token linear probe, scored pooled with 15-bin ECE, AUROC, and Brier, on the readout model's own generations.

It cannot match these, because they are unstated or unreleased:

- the PPO checkpoints;
- the probe layer;
- the R-Tuning hyperparameters for Sec 4;
- the LiveCodeBench window;
- the grader.

Each will be listed as a protocol deviation (`docs/s6/PIPELINE_DESIGN_R1.md` section 2, written in Part B of this wave).
