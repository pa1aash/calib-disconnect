# SWE-Router: trajectory-conditioned routing (2607.00053)
Status: concurrent (posted 2026-06-30). Source: research/notes/swe-router-routing-in-multi-turn-agentic-software-engineering-tasks.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: a cheap model runs a few turns in mini-SWE-agent; a learned value head reads the partial trajectory and predicts success to decide escalation to a strong model. Metrics: AUROC of the value head and Route-AUC, the normalized area under the cost versus resolved-rate curve (Sec. 5.1).
Models and benchmarks: weak gpt-5-mini or deepseek-v3.2, strong gemini-3-pro-preview; value head Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct with LoRA; SWE-Smith and SWE-Bench Verified.
Findings: Sec. 5.3: "Route-AUC of 0.780, which is a +15.3 pp improvement over the non-temporal routing baseline (K=0)" for deepseek-v3.2 on SWE-Bench Verified. Theorem 4.1: more informative signals improve Bayes-optimal routing. Sec. 5.3 notes value-head AUROC "not strongly correlated" with routing performance.
Leaves open: Router is a trained value head, not the generating model's verbal or hidden-state confidence. A random band is drawn in Fig. 2; no oracle-routing arm (searched: oracle). Routes between models, not execution budgets over candidate solutions. No verbal confidence update after test failures.
