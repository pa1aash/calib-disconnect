# Execution labels set conformal risk, text-to-SQL (2609.25938)
Status: concurrent (posted 2026-09-22; "Preprint"). Source: research/notes/certified-against-which-oracleexecution-labels-set-the-reported-riskof-conformal.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: preregistered swap of the execution oracle (shipped database vs distilled multi-instance test suite) and its effect on conformal abstention risk and on execution-consistency confidence scores, plus blinded expert labels (Sec. 3, Table 2).
Models and benchmarks: four SQL-specialist checkpoints from two Qwen lineages (Table 1); Spider-Realistic, 508 questions, 50 samples each at temperature 1.0.
Findings: Abstract: the swap "raises the certificate's held-out risk 2.73 to 10.23 points above the risk its own labels report"; expert labels give "20.0 and 17.2 points of risk" at nominal 0.10; "Every execution-consistency score looks better under the labels of the oracle that built its clusters, in 16 of 16 combinations."
Leaves open: Text-to-SQL, not general code. Supports that label oracle and label independence change reported confidence quality, which bears on L1 and F. No code-benchmark replication, no verbal, probe, or hidden-state readouts.
