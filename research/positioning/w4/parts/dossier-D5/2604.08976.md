# Quantisation reshapes metacognitive geometry (2604.08976)
Status: preprint only (2026-04-10). Source: research/notes/quantisation-reshapes-the-metacognitivegeometry-of-language-models.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: Type-2 signal-detection M-ratio and Type-2 AUROC per knowledge domain, Q5_K_M vs f16 quantisation; null domain-conditional SFT intervention (Sec. 3, Table 3).
Models and benchmarks: Llama-3-8B-Instruct; TriviaQA, 3,000 evaluation questions in four domains (Table 2).
Findings: "M-ratio profiles across four knowledge domains are uncorrelated between formats (Spearman rho=0.00)"; "Type-2 AUROC profiles are perfectly stable across formats (rho=1.00)" (Abstract). "All four confirmatory hypotheses were null."
Leaves open: Non-code, one model, factual QA. Shows a calibration-like metric (M-ratio) can depend on inference format while AUROC does not; no execution labels. Bears on F as an adjacent format-dependence precedent, not an occupant. Limitations: "one model at two quantisation levels".
