# Uncertainty and self-correction in code generation (2608.14659)
Status: concurrent (posted 2026-07-31; accepted to the ICML 2026 Workshop on Statistical Frameworks for Uncertainty in Agentic Systems). Source: research/notes/1introduction.md (misnamed; verified by arXiv id and title match). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: correlation of five uncertainty estimators with pass/fail, then three self-correction policies (adaptive decoding, uncertainty-triggered regeneration, verification-based regeneration) by Pass@1 and latency (Sec. 3.4, 4).
Models and benchmarks: Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen2.5-Coder-3B, DeepSeek-Coder-1.3B (instruct); HumanEval, BigCodeBench.
Findings: Table 1 (HumanEval) Pearson, Llama/Qwen/DeepSeek: P(True) 0.842/0.782/0.303; Verbalized Confidence -0.327/-0.090/-0.114; Semantic Entropy Probe -0.196/-0.475/-0.416. Abstract: uncertainty-based self-correction degrades Pass@1 "in 5 of 6 configurations (-3 pp to -10 pp)"; verification-based gains "+6 to +26" pp on HumanEval. Table 2 uses n=31 held-out problems.
Leaves open: L3 core claim (routing execution budgets against random and oracle) is not tested; no random or oracle routing arm (searched: oracle, random). Threshold-gated regeneration only.
