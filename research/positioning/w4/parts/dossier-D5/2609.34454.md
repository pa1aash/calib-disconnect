# IPoET: hidden-state estimator vs verbalized confidence (2609.34454)
Status: concurrent (posted 2026-09-28). Source: research/notes/when-words-fall-short-iterative-synergy-between-verbalized-reasoning-and-hidden.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: a hidden-state confidence estimator (linear head, last token, Brier loss) vs RL-trained verbalized confidence, then IPoET alternating policy and estimator training; AUROC, Brier, ECE (Sec. 1, 3, 4, Table 1, Fig. 1a).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3-8B-Base (main), Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct; trained on HotpotQA or Big-Math; in-domain and out-of-domain QA and math.
Findings: Abstract: "a dedicated confidence estimator can substantially outperform verbalized confidence"; Sec. 1: estimator overfitting "can bias comparisons in favor of verbalized confidence". Fig. 1(a) reports relative improvements over verbalized confidence.
