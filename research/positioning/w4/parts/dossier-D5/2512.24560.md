# Localized calibrated uncertainty in code LMs (2512.24560)
Status: preprint only (2025-12-31). Source: research/notes/localized-calibrated-uncertainty-in-code-language-models.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: calibrated probability that a token, line, or completion is edited in a minimal test-verified patch; probes vs token probability vs multisampling vs line-level verbalized reflection (Sec. 4 to 8).
Models and benchmarks: generators GPT-4o and Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct; probes on 0.5B and 7B supervisors; HumanEval+, MBPP+, LiveCodeBench, RepoCod-s (Table 2). Patches from o4-mini and Claude 4 Sonnet.
Findings: Table 4 (Qwen) line level, Probe-7B: unscaled BSS 0.29, scaled BSS 0.33, ECE 0.02; Line Verbalized unscaled BSS -0.72, scaled 0.10. Table 3 (GPT-4o) Probe-7B scaled BSS 0.22; Line Verbalized scaled 0.17.
Leaves open: Occupies per-line calibrated localization with probes vs verbal vs sampling on code. Repair-vs-regeneration and per-requirement groups: not present (searched: regenerat, requirement). Probes read a separate supervisor model, not the generator's own states.
