# Do LLMs know what they are capable of (2512.24661)
Status: preprint only (first posting December 2025 per arXiv id; no venue found). Source: research/notes/do-large-language-models-know-what-they-are-capable-of.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: verbal in-advance success probability on code tasks (Experiment 1), confidence and accept decisions over sequential contracts with in-context outcomes (Experiment 2), per-step confidence on SWE-bench Verified (Experiment 3).
Models and benchmarks: Llama, GPT, and Claude families; BigCodeBench, 1,140 tasks; SWE-bench Verified, 499 tasks with 70 tool calls each (Experiments 1 and 3).
Findings: "All LLMs we tested are overconfident, but most predict their success with better-than-random discriminatory power"; "reasoning LLMs perform comparably to or worse than non-reasoning LLMs"; "some but not all LLMs reduce their overconfidence" after in-context failures (Abstract).
Leaves open: verbal readout only (searched: probe, hidden, logit). Reasoning budget is 0 in Experiments 1 and 2; reasoning is on in Experiment 3. Updating follows prior-contract outcomes, not failing tests on the same code (Limitations and Future Directions).
