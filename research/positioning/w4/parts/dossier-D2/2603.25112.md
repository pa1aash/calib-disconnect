# SDT metacognitive efficiency of LLM confidence (2603.25112)
Status: preprint only (first posting March 2026 per arXiv id; v3 dated 28 Jul 2026). Source: research/notes/do-llms-know-what-they-know-measuring-metacognitive-efficiency-with-signal-detec.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: Type-2 signal detection structure of token-level normalized log-probability versus answer correctness, plus a model-free information measure meta-I_2r.
Models and benchmarks: Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Llama-3-8B-Base, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Gemma-2-9B-Instruct, Q5_K_M quantized; TriviaQA (5,000) and Natural Questions (3,000) (Table 1; Methods).
Findings: "metacognitive information varies by a factor of 1.98 across models"; z-ROC slopes "from 0.78 to 1.18" invisible to calibration metrics; rho = +1.00 between meta-I_2r and abstention gain (Abstract). v3 retracts the v1/v2 inverse accuracy-efficiency coupling after a length-biased scorer was found (version note).
Leaves open: no code, no execution labels, no hidden-state probe or verbal readout; meta-d' is reported ill-defined for open QA (Abstract). Bears on F (SDT and decomposition of the confidence signal exist) but not on L1 to L4.
Breadth:
- confidence families: 1 (token log-probability)
- model families: 3 (Meta, Mistral, Google)
- scales: 7B to 9B
- benchmarks: 2, factual QA, not code
- base and instruct pairs: 1 (Llama-3-8B base vs instruct, Table 1)
- post-training stages: not present (searched: SFT, RLHF, DPO, post-training)
- reasoning vs non-reasoning: not present (searched: reasoning, chain)
- elicitation formats: not present (single token-probability readout)
- label source: automated string match with containment fallback, validated on 1,200 human adjudications
- label independence: not present (searched: independent)
- contamination split: not present (searched: contamination, release date)
- calibration metrics: AUROC2, z-ROC slope, d_a, meta-I_2r with permutation null; ECE and Brier discussed
- granularity: per-answer
- causal intervention: not present (searched: steer, ablat, patch); temperature is varied
Bears on: F, component "metacognitive-efficiency formalization" (F question 4): NARROWS (type-2 SDT applied to LLM log-probability exists; the measurand-readout decomposition must differ). L1 to L4: no effect.
