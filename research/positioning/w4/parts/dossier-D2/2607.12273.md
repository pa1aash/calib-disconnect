# Code-MUE execution-graph uncertainty (2607.12273)
Status: published (PACMSE vol. 3, ISSTA 2026, article ISSTA185, DOI 10.1145/3832276 per the paper header); concurrent (arXiv id dates it July 2026, after 2026-05-10). Source: research/notes/code-mue-measuring-code-llms-uncertainty-through-execution-based-semantic-intera.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: black-box Von Neumann entropy of an execution-based semantic interaction graph over sampled programs, versus functional correctness (Section 5.2).
Models and benchmarks: gpt-4.1-mini, gpt-4.1-nano, gemini-2.5-flash-lite, Claude-3-Haiku, Llama 3.1-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, DeepSeek-Coder-6.7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct; HumanEval, MBPP (200 subset), QuixBugs, CodeNet (200 subset) (Section 5.1, Table 2).
Findings: "Spearman's correlation up to -0.98" and "AUROC scores exceeding 0.85 on multiple b[enchmarks]" (Abstract; Section 1). 100 samples per question at T = 1.0 (Section 5.1; Threats).
Leaves open: verbal confidence and hidden states excluded by design. The AUROC label "Solved" means at least one of the N sampled candidates passes all tests, so the label shares the samples used by the method (Section 5.2.1).
Breadth:
- confidence families: sampling agreement (execution graph), logit, lexical, embedding; no verbal or probe
- model families: 7 vendors (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Mistral, DeepSeek, Alibaba)
- scales: small, 6.7B to 8B open plus API models
- benchmarks: 4, four tasks
- base and instruct pairs: not present (searched: base model; all instruction-tuned)
- post-training stages: not present
- reasoning vs non-reasoning: not present (searched: reasoning model, thinking)
- elicitation formats: not present
- label source: benchmark tests
- label independence: not independent (label from the same N samples)
- contamination split: not present (searched: contamin, release date, cutoff)
- calibration metrics: Spearman, AUROC; ECE not used (searched: ECE, Brier)
- granularity: whole solution
- causal intervention: not present
Bears on: L1 sampling-agreement family: NARROWS (execution-based agreement with AUROC exists across 8 models and 4 benchmarks, single family). L2, L3: no effect.
