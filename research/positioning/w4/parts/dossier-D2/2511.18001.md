# TokenRepair token-level repair (2511.18001)
Status: preprint only (first posting November 2025 per arXiv id; no venue found in text). Source: research/notes/enhancing-automated-program-repair-via-faulty-token-localization-and-quality-awa.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: token-level uncertainty fluctuation as a suspiciousness score to localize faulty tokens in a generated patch, then token-guided CoT rewriting plus quality-aware filtering, versus test-feedback regeneration baselines such as ChatRepair (Sections 3 to 5).
Models and benchmarks: five 7-8B instruct code models; Defects4J 1.2 (154 single-hunk bugs) and HumanEval-Java (163 pairs), with the buggy hunk location given from ground truth (Section 5.1).
Findings: "correctly fixing 88 bugs on Defects4J 1.2 and 139 bugs on HumanEval-Java", gains "8.2% to 34.9%" and "3.3% to 16.1%" (Abstract). Ablation: removing uncertainty-guided localization "could drop the performance by 20.6%" (Answer to RQ3). RQ1: all three metrics "exhibit strong correlations with patch correctness".
Leaves open: no ECE or AUROC (searched: ECE, AUROC, calibration); confidence is token-level, not per requirement or per test group; no verbal or probe readout. The targeted-rewrite versus regeneration comparison is at token granularity, with the faulty hunk given.
