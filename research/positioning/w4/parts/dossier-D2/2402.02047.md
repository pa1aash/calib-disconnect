# Calibration and correctness of language models for code (2402.02047)
Status: published (ICSE 2025, per the vault note summary; the full text carries no venue string). Source: research/notes/calibration-and-correctness-of-language-models-for-code.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: calibration of code-model confidence against correctness at token, line, and function level, plus repair (Section IV, RQ1 to RQ4).
Models and benchmarks: gpt-3.5-turbo-instruct, Codex, CodeGen2-16B (Section III); HumanEval (164), MBPP, line-level completion, ManySStubs4J, Defects4J (120) (Section III, Threats to Validity).
Findings: intrinsic confidences have "ECE ... ranging from 0.09 to 0.73" (Section I); "While rescaling generally improves calibration, reflective methods are rather inconsistent" (Section I).
