# LLMs as Signal Detectors (2603.14893)
Status: preprint only (v1 16 Mar 2026). Source: research/notes/llms-as-signal-detectors-sensitivity-bias-and-the-temperaturecriterion-analogy.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: pre-registered unequal-variance signal detection fit (d_a, criterion c, z-ROC slope) of normalized answer log-probability versus correct/incorrect, across seven temperatures (Sections 2.2, 3.3).
Models and benchmarks: Llama-3-8B-Instruct, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Llama-3-8B-Base, Q5_K_M (Section 3.1); TriviaQA and Natural Questions (Section 3.2).
Findings: "z-ROC slope was below 1.0 in all 42 conditions" (Section 4); instruct slopes 0.52-0.63, base 0.77-0.87 (abstract); models with similar ECE occupy different (d_a, c) positions (Section 4). Temperature changes both sensitivity and criterion.
Leaves open: no code, no execution labels, no hidden-state or verbal readout. For F, it supplies a bias-versus-sensitivity split of one readout, not a measurand-readout split.
Breadth:

- Model families: two (Llama, Mistral).
- Scales: 7-8B only (Section 5.5).

- Base and instruct pairs: one (Llama-3-8B).
