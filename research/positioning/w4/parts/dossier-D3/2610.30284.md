# SWE-Verifier (2610.30284)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 21 Oct 2026, preprint only). Source: research/notes/261030284-swe-verifier-selective-verification-for-coding-agents-full-text.md (17.6k words). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: a trained Qwen3.5-4B step scorer that gates candidate-action sampling and ranks actions in mini-swe-agent; resolve rate and total cost versus no-verifier, trajectory-level, and all-step verification (Section 2, Table 1).
Models and benchmarks: policies MiniMax-M2.7 and DeepSeek-V4-Flash (reasoning set to "correct-high", Table 1 caption); SWE-Bench Verified, 500 tasks.
Findings: MiniMax 75.47 to 77.20 resolve at 1.09x cost with 3.43% of steps verified; DeepSeek 72.60 to 74.27 at 1.13x (Table 1). Trajectory Oracle@3: 82.20 (MiniMax) and 80.60 (DeepSeek) at 3.00x cost.
Leaves open: the gate is a trained verifier preference, not elicited, verbal, or probed confidence, and no calibration metric is reported. Oracle@3 is best-of-three rollouts, not oracle routing of a budget. No confidence-update-after-failure measurement.
