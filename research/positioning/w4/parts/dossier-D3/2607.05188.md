# Latent Programming Horizons in Coding Agents (2607.05188)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 6 Jul 2026, preprint only). Source: research/notes/260705188-latent-programming-horizons-in-coding-agents-full-text.md (11k words). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: logistic-regression probes on residual streams of coding agents (mini-swe-agent) decoding the current program's parse status, test-suite pass, failing-test reduction, and regression, plus probes of future-edit outcomes (Sections 3, 4.1).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Laguna-XS.2; SWE-bench Verified and SWE-bench Pro (731 tasks), up to 10 trajectories per task (Table 1, Section 3).
Findings: "AUC up to 0.83" for full correctness, 0.84 partial correctness for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (Section 4.1); Laguna-XS.2 up to 0.73 (Verified) and regression 0.75 (Pro); future-edit probes above chance "up to roughly 25 steps in advance"; probes transfer across benchmarks (abstract).
Leaves open: no verbal, token-probability, or sampling comparison; steering "left to future work" (Section 6); two models, one scaffold.
