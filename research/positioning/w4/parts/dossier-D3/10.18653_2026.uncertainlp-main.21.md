# Verbalized Uncertainty in Coding LLMs (10.18653/2026.uncertainlp-main.21)
Status: published (Third Workshop on Uncertainty-Aware NLP, 29 Oct 2026, pp. 328-355; workshop tag). Source: research/notes/verbalized-uncertainty-in-coding-llms-contamination-aware-calibration-uncertainl.md (larger copy). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: verbalized confidence under Direct, Chain-of-Thought, and Linguistic prompts on frozen greedy pass@1 code, against hidden-test labels (Sections 3.3, 3.5).
Models and benchmarks: six instruct coder models (Qwen2.5-Coder 7B/32B, CodeLlama 7B/34B, DeepSeek-Coder 6.7B/33B), BF16; 365 LiveCodeBench v6 problems released 2024-09-19 to 2025-04-30 (Sections 3.1, 3.2).
Findings: "All eighteen strict-scoring NCE point estimates are negative" (Section 4.2); lowest ECE 0.013 (CodeLlama-7B Linguistic) has AUROC 0.491; highest AUROC 0.841 (Qwen-32B Linguistic) has ECE 0.216 (Table 1). Strict accuracy 8.22% to 29.86% (Section 4.1).
Leaves open: no hidden-state, token-probability, or sampling readout; one shared window rather than per-model post-cutoff splits; no base or reasoning models.
Breadth:
- Confidence families: one (verbalized), three prompt formats.
- Model families: three.
- Scales: two per family (6.7B-34B).
- Benchmarks: one (LiveCodeBench v6).
- Base and instruct pairs: not present (searched: base model, pretrained).
- Post-training stages: not present.
- Reasoning vs non-reasoning: not present (searched: reasoning model; CoT prompt only).
- Elicitation formats: three (Section 3.3).
- Label source: hidden-test execution (Section 3.1).
- Label independence: yes, code frozen before elicitation (Section 3.3).
- Contamination split: shared post-cutoff window, Qwen date is a proxy (Limitations).
- Calibration metrics: ECE (10 equal-width bins), NCE, AUROC, AUACC, PRR, Platt diagnostic.
- Granularity: whole solution.
- Causal intervention: not present.
