# Falsification, Not Exposure, placebo-controlled self-repair decomposition (2606.31511)
Status: preprint only (arXiv 2606.31511v1, 30 Jun 2026; concurrent, posted after 2026-05-10). Source: research/notes/falsification-not-exposure-placebo-controlled-decomposition-of-self-repair-feedb.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: a preregistered decomposition of self-repair feedback packets (bare failing code, executed facts, code plus facts, instruction-only, content-free placebos) against blind resampling at matched generation budget; "true unlock" requires passing public and prompt-hidden EvalPlus tiers (Abstract, Sec 2).
Models and benchmarks: deepseek-coder 1.3B, qwen2.5-coder 0.5B and 1.5B, frozen; HumanEval+ and MBPP+; 290 dead units, 7,000 generations plus 1,400 follow-up (Sec 2.1.1, Abstract).
Findings: "Blind resampling exceeded bare-code retry by +18 net unlocks (discordant 25/7, Holm-adjusted p = 0.0021)"; code-plus-facts and blind resampling "tied at the pooled level with 26 unlocks each" (Abstract).
Leaves open: no confidence, probability, or calibration measurement (searched: confidence, calibrat, verbal, logprob; the only hits are statistical confidence bounds), so L3's question of whether stated confidence updates after a failing test is untouched. For L4 it supplies a matched-budget repair-versus-resample result at the whole-solution level, not per-requirement.
