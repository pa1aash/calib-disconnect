# Wired for Overconfidence, circuit analysis of verbalized confidence (2604.01457)
Status: accepted (COLM 2026; v3 posted 27 Jul 2026). Source: research/notes/wired-for-overconfidence-a-mechanistic-perspective-on-inflated-verbalized-confid.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: circuit-level mechanistic analysis of inflated verbalized confidence: a TSLD proxy, edge attribution patching, a truth-injection counterfactual, and two interventions (mean ablation, activation steering) on the top-10 components (Sec 2 to 4, Fig 1).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct and Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct as scale check (App.); PopQA, NQOpen, MMLU (Sec 2). Factual QA, no code.
Findings: "Ablating the circuit eliminates 74-79% of the baseline TSLD across all four settings" (Sec 3, Table 2); steering at alpha = 0.6 lowers ECE "from 0.647 to 0.068" on Qwen2.5-7B PopQA (Table 9).
Leaves open: the only causal intervention on a stated-confidence circuit found in this batch (L2 question 12), but on factual QA with instruct models only, no execution labels, no probe-versus-verbal comparison, no base pair.
