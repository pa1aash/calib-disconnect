# Pareto Optimal Code Generation, staged verification (2506.10056)
Status: preprint only (arXiv v1 June 2025 per id; v2 dated 24 Feb 2026). Source: research/notes/1introduction-3.md (misnamed; full HTML text). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: the accuracy-throughput Pareto frontier of verifiers in generate-then-rank code pipelines: full test suite, partial or random test subsets, outcome reward models (ORMs), and staged verification where cheap filters precede ORM ranking (Abstract, Sec 1, Fig 1, Table 1).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen 2.5 Coder Instruct generators (500M to 14B) and Qwen-backbone ORMs trained on CodeContests-Python and GSM8K; four benchmarks incl. CodeContests, HumanEval, MBPP (Sec 1, 3).
Findings: "ORMs with staged verification shift the Pareto frontier outward, achieving 11.64x higher throughput at a cost of 8.26% accuracy relative to full test-suite verification" (Abstract); random test subsets give false-positive rates comparable to curated ones (Sec 5).
Leaves open: routing is by filter-then-rank, not by calibrated confidence; no oracle-routing or random-routing bound on budget; no calibration of verifier scores (L3 questions 1, 4).
