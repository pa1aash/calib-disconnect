# Semantic-distance uncertainty for code, SDE and DSDE (2605.09023)
Status: preprint only (arXiv 2605.09023v1, 9 May 2026). Source: research/notes/using-semantic-distance-to-estimate-uncertainty-in-llm-based-code-generation.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: sample-based uncertainty estimators that cluster K=10 sampled programs by execution behavior on N=10 fuzz inputs and add graded distances (Sec 2, Table 1 taxonomy; Sec 4.1).
Models and benchmarks: GPT-3.5-Turbo, GPT-4o-mini, Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite, Claude Opus 4.5 (pass@1 30.83%, 49.24%, 60.53%, 82.73% on LiveCodeBench); DeepSeek-Coder-V2 (38.14%) for generalization; LCB, MBPP, HumanEval-X, BigCodeBench (Sec 4.1).
Findings: "DSDE achieves AUROC above 0.8 in every setting" (Sec 4.2, Table 2). Metrics: AUROC for pass@1, Pearson and Spearman with partial_pass@1.
Leaves open: no verbal, token-probability, or hidden-state readouts, so no cross-family comparison (L1 questions 1, 2); no release-date split; no calibration error; one open-weights model (L1, L2).
Breadth:
- confidence families: sampling agreement (execution-behavior clustering) only; baselines are other sample-based estimators (Sec 4.1, RQ1)
- model families: OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, DeepSeek
- scales: not reported (closed APIs); pass@1 tiers low to high
- benchmarks: LCB, MBPP, HumanEval-X (Python, Java, C++), BigCodeBench
- base and instruct pairs: not present (searched: base, instruct)
