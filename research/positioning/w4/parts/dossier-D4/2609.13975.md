# Introspective Uncertainty Estimation for code, Klassert thesis (2609.13975)
Status: preprint only (arXiv 2609.13975v1, 12 Sep 2026; MSc thesis submitted 1 May 2026, Hochschule RheinMain). Source: research/notes/abstract.md (misnamed). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: hidden-state probes for response-level and line-level code correctness (Abstract, Sec 4.4, Ch 5).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3-Coder, GPT-OSS-20B, NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Nano, Olmo 3.1 Instruct 32B (Table 4.2); LiveCodeBench (1055 tasks) and BigCodeBench-Instruct (Sec 4.1).
Findings: "0.90 AUROC and 0.96 F1 on LCB in the best settings" (Abstract); on CALIBRI (Qwen3-Coder, LCB) probe BSS 0.5888 vs IGLB 0.4680 (Table 5.7); Top-3 hit rate 81% for conditional line localization, fix-then-diff labels audited at about 90% agreement.
Leaves open: no verbal, token-probability, or sampling readout of its own, so no probe-versus-verbal gap (L2); contamination explicitly not controlled (Sec 4.1, "prioritizes ... over strict contamination control") (L1); line-level labels are synthetic edits, not test-group confidence (L4).
