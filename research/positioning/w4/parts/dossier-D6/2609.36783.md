# A Heckler in the Hidden State (2609.36783)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v2 dated 2026-09-30, preprint only; v1 date not in text). Source: research/notes/a-heckler-in-the-hidden-statecorrectness-signals-in-diffusion-language-models.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: difference-of-means probe direction (pass minus fail, response-pooled residual stream) read as within-problem AUC; comparison with model confidence; steering along the probe direction (Sections 3.2, 3.3, 4).
Models and benchmarks: six diffusion code models (DiffuCoder-7B, Dream-Coder-7B, Stable-DiffCoder-8B, UltraLLaDA-8B, LLaDA2.0-mini, LLaDA-flash) plus Qwen2.5-Coder; MBPP+ and HumanEval+ via EvalPlus (Section 3.1).
Findings: "DiffuCoder gives a probe AUC of 0.842, compared with 0.838 for negative token entropy"; Qwen2.5-Coder "probe AUC is 0.677 and log-probability confidence AUC is 0.703" (Section 4.2, Table 1). Steering pass@1 "0.433 without intervention ... 0.388 when pushed toward the passing direction, and 0.298 when pushed toward the failing direction" (Section 4.4).
Leaves open: small cohorts (19 mixed problems in the 32-step comparison, Section 3.1); no verbal readout; no base/instruct; causal test is steering of generation, not of stated confidence.
