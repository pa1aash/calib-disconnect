# Hidden-state probes for localizing reasoning failures (2610.22046)
Status: preprint only (arXiv v1 2026-10-15; workshop template "Interpretability as a Science"). Source: research/notes/effectiveness-of-hidden-state-probesfor-localizing-reasoning-failures.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: probes on hidden states trained with final labels, Monte Carlo rollout rates, or the pit step, to separate capability gap, near miss, and pit (single-step unrecoverable) traces and to locate the failing step, against non-hidden-state baselines (Sections 1-4).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B in thinking mode; DeepMath-103k and Hendrycks MATH train split (Section 1). Math, not code.
Findings: abstract: the confidence probe "misses 81% of cases in which a single step makes the trace unrecoverable and 47% of cases in which another attempt would succeed" (8B). Detection AUROC "0.814 and 0.809 over 5 seeds"; ECE "0.014 / 0.024" (Table 13). AUROC of pits against near misses: confidence probe 0.76, combined probe 0.40 (Finding 2).
Leaves open: no code and no execution labels (labels are rollout-based). No verbal confidence readout is measured; a verbal-confidence claim is only cited from Zhao et al. 2026a.
