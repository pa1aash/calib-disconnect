# Metacognitive sensitivity for dynamic model selection (2512.10451)
Status: preprint only (2025-12-11); template header names the CogInterp workshop. Source: research/notes/metacognitive-sensitivity-for-test-timedynamic-model-selection.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: meta-d' (type-2 sensitivity) of a model's confidence, computed on a sliding 100-trial window and fed as bandit context (LinUCB or LinTS) to pick one of two models per input (Sections 3, 4, Algorithm 1).
Models and benchmarks: AlexNet, GoogleNet, EfficientNet, ViT on CIFAR-10; CLIP, ALIGN, SigLIP, MetaCLIP on PACS plus CIFAR-10 (Section 5). No code, no LLM text.
Findings: "AlexNet-ViT 62.4 69.5 (+7.1%)" at 300 trials; gains of 1.4%-3.5% at 1000 trials (Table 1). VLM gain 0.3%-1.8% (Section 5).
Leaves open: meta-d' is used as a routing signal, not a code-confidence measurand. Extension to LLMs is listed only as future work (Conclusion).
