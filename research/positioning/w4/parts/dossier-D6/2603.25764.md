# Confident and Wrong: silent semantic failures in coding agents (2603.25764)

Measured: submit rate (indicator) against test-verified resolve rate (criterion) for agents over 5 repeats; reliability versus validity grid; an abstention probe with the gold patch pre-applied (Sections 2, 5, 6).
Models and benchmarks: Claude 4.5 Sonnet, GPT-5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, Llama 4 Maverick; 50 SWE-bench Verified tasks, mini-SWE-agent, temperature 0.5 (Section 4).
