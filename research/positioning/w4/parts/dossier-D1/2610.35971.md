# Continue or restart, early failure prediction in coding agents (2610.35971)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 posted 2026-10-26). Source: research/notes/continue-or-restart-the-limits-of-early-failure-prediction-in-coding-agents.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.

Measured: whether early prefixes of agent runs predict outcome within a task, and how score-guided restarts compare with matched random restarts and uninterrupted continuation under token budgets. Models and benchmarks: 147,002 traces, four settings: OpenHands with Qwen3-Coder-480B-A35B-Instruct on SWE-rebench; SWE-agent and OpenHands with Qwen3.5-122B-A10B; OpenHands with MiniMax-M2.5 (Section III-A, Table I). Findings: within-task AUC "0.50 [0.48, 0.51]" at 20k tokens; "learned restarting improves resolution over matched random restarting by up to 1.98 percentage points" under a 75k cap; the gain is between-task allocation (within-task -0.11 to +0.24 pp); continuation wins "216 of 224 tested budget-threshold configurations" (Abstract). Leaves open: predictors are count models, embeddings, and a fine-tuned Qwen3-0.6B monitor, not model confidence; no verbal update after failing tests (searched: verbaliz, stated).
