# Characterizing overconfident failure in code generation (2610.11300)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 posted 2026-10-08). Source: research/notes/characterizing-overconfident-failure-in-llm-based-code-generation-full-text.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.

Measured: how well 17 generation-stage and 6 prefill-stage uncertainty scores separate passing from failing code (AUC, fail positive); selective generation; base vs instruct; Platt and isotonic calibration; prompted abstention; hidden-state effective rank. Models and benchmarks: CodeLlama, DeepSeek-Coder, OpenCoder, Qwen2.5-Coder (7B to 8B, instruct); HumanEval+, MBPP+ (BigCodeBench excluded for low pass rates); up to 10 samples at temperature 1.0 (Section 4). Findings: Table 3 AUCs span 0.42 to 0.70 in the extracted text; instruction tuning on MBPP: DeepSeekCoder confidence AUC 0.4391 to 0.5654, dAUC +0.1263, 95% CI [0.0447, 0.2089]; OpenCoder dAUC -0.0136, CI [-0.0911, 0.0652] (Section 5.5). Leaves open: no trained probe or verbal confidence, so L2 probe vs verbal stays open; base vs instruct covers two models, one benchmark, token-based readouts.
