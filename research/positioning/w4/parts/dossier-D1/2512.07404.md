# Ribeiro et al., internal representation of code correctness (2512.07404)
Status: published or accepted (ICSE 2026, pp. 1160-1172, per research/positioning/w4/L2_OCCUPANT.md; v3 2026-01-21). Source: research/notes/on-llms-internal-representation-of-code-correctness.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.

Measured: a Representation Engineering (LAT) hidden-state direction vs sequence likelihood and prompted verbal confidence, as a selector among candidate programs. Models and benchmarks: Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct, OpenCoder-8B-Instruct, CodeLlama-7B-Instruct (Section 4.1.4); HumanEval (151 tasks), BigCodeBench (457 tasks); unit-test labels. Findings: "On BigCodeBench, it improves accuracy by +13.3 to +29.0 pp over the best reflective accuracy; on HumanEval, gains range from +8.7 to +26.2 pp over the strongest baseline" (Section 4.2.1); verbal formats disagree, Mistral HumanEval 40.6% (Regular) vs 15.3% (True/False). Leaves open: where the gap arises, sampling-agreement readouts, calibration metrics, sub-solution granularity.
