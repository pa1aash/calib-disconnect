# Symbolic Equivalence Partitioning, SEP (2604.06485)
Status: preprint only (v2 dated 2026-05-11; v1 date not in the text, the id indicates April 2026, so likely not concurrent). Source: research/notes/inference-time-code-selection-via-symbolic-equivalence-partitioning-full-text.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: best-of-N selection accuracy. Public-example filter, then bounded symbolic execution partitions candidates into equivalence classes and the dominant class is chosen (Section 3).
Models and benchmarks: Phi-4-mini-instruct, Phi-4-reasoning, Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, Qwen3-14B, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, gpt-oss-20b; HumanEval+ (164) and LiveCodeBench (438 filtered) (Section 4.1).
Findings: "At N = 10, SEP improves average accuracy from 0.754 to 0.826 on HumanEval+ and from 0.565 to 0.647 on LiveCodeBench" (abstract; Tables 1, 2).
Leaves open: SEP is a selector, not a confidence score. Related symbolic work (Sharma and David) is abstention-oriented. No calibration or routing against random and oracle budgets.
