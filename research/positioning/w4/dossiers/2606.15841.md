# Heteroskedastic signals in budgeted LLM verification (2606.15841)
Status: concurrent (arXiv id 2606, v2 dated 2026-07-20; preprint only). Source: research/notes/heteroskedastic-signals-in-budgeted-llm-verificationstructural-heterogeneity-lim.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: hit rate (share of audited outputs that are errors) under a verification budget, for Random, global Threshold, MP-Adapt, MP-Strat, cost-stratified thresholding (CST), and Oracle policies on token-level uncertainty proxies (Sections 3, 5).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3-8B, LLaMA3-8B, GPT-4o-mini; MBPP (N=500, unit-test feedback) and MATH (N=5000) (Section 5.1).
Findings: MBPP Qwen3, beta=20%: Rand .546, Thr .610, CST .661, Oracle 1.00 (Table 1). CST minus Threshold "+3.4" to "+17.0" pp (Introduction). Setup adds "10% Bernoulli label noise and Gaussian proxy noise" (Section 5.1).
Leaves open: proxies are mean log-probability and logit-margin features only; no verbal or probe confidence; no calibration or discrimination metric; simulated stream with injected noise; no stated-confidence update after failing tests.
