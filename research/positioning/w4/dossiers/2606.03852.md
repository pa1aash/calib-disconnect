# FLARE: line-level diagnostic feedback for refinement (2606.03852)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 2026-06-02, preprint only). Source: research/notes/flare-fine-grained-diagnostic-feedback-for-llm-code-refinement.md (copy with full text; the 260603852-* note is abstract-sized). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: line-level suspiciousness from a small diagnostic model fed the coder LLM's token probabilities (aligned to AST lexical units, Eqs. 1-3, Section 2.1), then top-k line-targeted refinement with candidate choice by visible-test outcome (Section 2.2).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3-8B, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.3-70B, GPT-4o-mini; LiveCodeBench and BigCodeBench; diagnostic model trained on Collu-Bench (Section 3.1).
Findings: Table 2 (LCB, BCB): "Execution feedback only (k=1)" 50.27, 53.81; "Fine-grained feedback only (k=1)" 50.86, 50.35; "Flare (k=1)" 54.86, 58.51; "Flare (k=10)" 65.14, 69.65. Diagnostic model "67% Top-1 and 89% Top-10 localization accuracy" (Introduction).
Leaves open: no calibration or AUROC, no per-test-group unit, no comparison of targeted repair guided by the model's own per-requirement confidence versus regeneration, no test-adequacy link. Hidden tests used only for final evaluation (Section 2.2).
