# Measurement validity of confidence signals (2610.25846)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 posted 2026-10-19). Source: research/notes/measurement-validity-and-representation-dynamics-ofconfidence-signals-in-large-l.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.

Measured: pre-registered audit of verbal, hidden-state probe, and sampling-agreement confidence; Murphy decomposition of Brier. Models and benchmarks: five Qwen2.5 models, 0.5B to 7B (one base and instruct pair at 7B); PopQA, SimpleQA, GSM8K, MATH; 500 questions per cell (Section 3.1). Findings: "Behavioral consensus leads on resolution: 2.02x that of verbal confidence (0.06371 vs. 0.03160)" (Section 6, Table 3, N=842); three verbal formats correlate weakly (Section 5, Table 2); probe onset 6.82% depth (retrieval) vs about 28% (reasoning) (Section 4, Table 1). Leaves open: no code, no execution labels; relevant to F only as a Murphy-decomposition and format-fragility precedent.
