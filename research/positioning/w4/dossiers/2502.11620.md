# Symbolic clustering uncertainty for code (2502.11620)
Status: preprint only (first posting February 2025 per arXiv id; no venue found in text). Source: research/notes/assessing-correctness-in-llm-based-code-generation-via-uncertainty-estimation.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: Pearson correlation of NLG-derived uncertainty (semantic entropy, mutual information, log-probabilities), with and without symbolic-execution clustering, against correctness; abstention policies (Sections 4 and 5; Table 2).
Models and benchmarks: DeepSeek-R1, gpt-3.5-turbo-instruct, claude-3-7-sonnet; LiveCodeBench, 831 problems (Section 5).
Findings: abstention "false positive rate below 0.02%, ... with false negative rates ranging from 11.9% to 20.3%" (Section 1); token log-probabilities "have limited effect: assuming a uniform output distribution performs comparably" (Section 1).
Leaves open: no verbal confidence, no probe, no calibration metric (ECE, AUROC not used); correctness is the percent of test cases passed by the top-ranked response (Section 5). Limitations: bounded symbolic execution; only Kuhn et al. and Abbasi et al. methods (Limitations).
