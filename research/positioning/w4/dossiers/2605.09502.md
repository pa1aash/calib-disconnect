# Hidden error awareness in CoT, diagnostic not causal (2605.09502)
Status: preprint only (first posting May 2026 per arXiv id) (month alone does not decide the concurrent window; the exact date was not checked). Source: research/notes/hidden-error-awareness-in-chain-of-thought-reasoningthe-signal-is-diagnostic-not.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: linear hidden-state probes versus verbalized confidence and other detectors for trace correctness during math chain of thought, plus four interventions (Abstract; Tables 1 and 2).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen2.5 (1.5B to 72B), Llama-3.1-8B, Phi-3.5-mini, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B (Table 1); MATH-500, 100 problems for training and 200 held out (Appendix B; Table 2). Not code.
Findings: probe "0.953" versus self-consistency N=5 "0.823", P(True) "0.721", sequence log-prob "0.676", verbalized "0.674" AUROC (Table 2, Qwen2.5-3B, n=200); verbalized confidence "4.55/5" for wrong versus "4.87/5" for correct traces (Abstract). Steering, probe-guided best-of-N, self-correction, and patching "all fail" (Abstract).
Leaves open: no code, no execution labels. Limitations: MATH-500 primary; no fine-tuning on probe signals (Limitations).
