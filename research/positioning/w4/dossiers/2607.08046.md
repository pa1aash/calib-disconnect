# What LLM forecasters know but do not say (2607.08046)
Status: concurrent (posted 2026-07-09). Source: research/notes/what-llm-forecasters-know-but-dont-say-probing-internal-representations-for-cali.md (12,021 words; the 260708046-* copy has 1,040). Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: representation-pooling probes on activations vs verbalized probability for forecast correctness; CoT faithfulness by evidence ablation and injection; pre-reasoning routing (Sec. 3, 4, Tables 1, 2).
Models and benchmarks: Eternis-Forecaster 8B (RLVR-trained from Qwen3-8B), GLM-4.7-Flash, GLM-4.5-Air; OpenForesight forecasting; AIME24/25 and AMC23/24 math stress test.
Findings: Table 1 (GLM-4.7-Flash): Probe AUROC 0.768 vs Verbal AUROC 0.669; Probe ECE 0.054 vs Verbal ECE 0.287. GLM-4.5-Air: 0.783 vs 0.691; ECE 0.102 vs 0.255. Table 2 (math OOD, untrained Qwen3-8B): ECE 0.083 vs best non-probe 0.270. Abstract: routing by pre-reasoning answer spread "saves 30-47% of generated tokens, with no loss of accuracy".
Leaves open: Probe-beats-verbal for reasoning models exists outside code, with LLM-judge labels, not execution. No base-vs-instruct pairs (searched: base model, instruct). The reasoning-token routing is a compute-allocation analogue to L3, not execution budgets.
