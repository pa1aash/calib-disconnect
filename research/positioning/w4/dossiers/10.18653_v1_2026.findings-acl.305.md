# NeuroSym-Cal hierarchical calibration for reasoning-model code (10.18653/v1/2026.findings-acl.305)
Status: published (Findings of ACL, 2026, pp. 6132-6143). Source: research/notes/neurosym-cal-bridging-the-reasoning-execution-gap-in-code-generation-via-hierarc.md (abstract only); full text fetched from aclanthology.org to raw/neurosym.pdf. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Mismatch: the vault summary says latent-perturbation "Reasoning Sensitivity Analysis"; the PDF's final method is prompted Self-Verification, with latent probing mentioned only as earlier exploration (Limitations).
Measured: a Contextual Calibration Network (3-layer MLP) fusing AST-consensus across K=10 samples with a self-verification score, against logit, P(True), self-consistency, and multicalibration baselines (Sec 3, 4.2).
Models and benchmarks: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B, Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct, Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct; LiveCodeBench v2406-v2506 (400 problems), HumanEval-Pro, DS-1000 (Sec 4.1).
Findings: "reducing Expected Calibration Error (ECE) by 37.4% compared to Self-Consistency (0.072 vs. 0.115) for DeepSeek-R1"; LCB AUROC 0.805 vs IGLB 0.798 (Sec 5.1); AURC down 6.7% vs IGLB (Sec 5.2).
