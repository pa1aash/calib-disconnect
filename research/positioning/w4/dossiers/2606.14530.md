# Pre-generation linear probe for code correctness (2606.14530)
Status: preprint only (first posting June 2026 per arXiv id; v3 dated 16 Jul 2026); concurrent (after 2026-05-10). Source: research/notes/code-correctness-is-linearly-decodable-from-llm-hidden-states-before-generation.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: whether a linear probe on the final prompt-token hidden state, captured before any output token, predicts pass or fail of the first attempt (Sections 2 and 3).
Models and benchmarks: Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 only; 444 LiveCodeBench tasks, easy to hard (Section 2.1).
Findings: "leakage-free held-out AUC of 0.881 ± 0.008 across 50 outer splits"; after residualizing prompt length "AUC of 0.842 ± 0.010", against a prompt-length baseline of "0.657 ± 0.014" (Abstract; Tables 3 and 4). Of 266 first-attempt failures, only 20 ever recovered in repair (Section 4).
Leaves open: single model (Limitations, "Single model"); no verbal, token-probability, or sampled readout; no ECE or Brier (searched: verbal, ECE, Brier, calibration); no steering. Bears on L2 probe training and pre-generation position (L2 question 5).
