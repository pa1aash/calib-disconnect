# Measuring (some aspects of) the metacognition of AI (2603.29693)
Status: preprint only (arXiv id 2603, v4 dated 2026-09-28; v1 date not verified). Source: research/notes/measuring-some-aspects-of-the-metacognition-of-ai.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: metacognitive sensitivity via meta-d' and M-ratio versus type-2 AUROC (AUC2), and type-1 criterion shifts under prompted risk configurations (Intro, Results).
Models and benchmarks: GPT-5 (reasoning effort Medium), DeepSeek-V3.2-Exp (non-thinking), Mistral-Medium-2508; three binary tasks: sentiment, oral vs written, deleted "the".
Findings: Paper is "primarily a methodological contribution" showing common measures of metacognitive sensitivity are confounded by type-1 performance (Abstract). "every model exhibits significant variations across tasks" in meta-d' (Results). Models' meta-d' "always suboptimal" (Results). Paper says "our article is not a benchmark paper" (Discussion).
Leaves open: Non-code, non-execution labels, API models only. Occupies the psychophysics framing of confidence sensitivity as a measurement problem. Does not address code, hidden states, or metrology (traceability, uncertainty budgets).
Breadth:

- Model families: OpenAI, DeepSeek, Mistral.
- Scales: not stated.
