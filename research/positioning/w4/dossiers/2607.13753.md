# Post-training shifts CoT confidence (2607.13753)
Status: concurrent (v2 dated 2026-07-20). Source: research/notes/post-training-shifts-confidence-a-three-stage-analysis-of-how-sft-rl-and-opd-sha.md. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: token-probability confidence before, during, and after chain-of-thought for SFT, RL, and on-policy-distillation variants, via AUROC and PRR (Sec. 3, 4).
Models and benchmarks: four models from one Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct backbone (Instruct, SFT, RL, OPD); AIME24, AIME25, AMC23, MATH500 (Table 1).
Findings: Table 1 Pre-CoT AUROC average for Qwen-Instruct is 0.594. Abstract: "OPD provides the most useful pre-reasoning confidence, SFT gives the strongest online signal for early stopping, and RL produces the most reliable trace-level signal"; PosConf "improves RL answer aggregation by 6.1 points over majority voting".
Leaves open: Post-training stage as a factor for confidence readouts exists on math only. Limitations: "mathematical reasoning benchmarks and models derived from a single Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct backbone"; only token-probability confidence.
