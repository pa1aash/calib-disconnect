# Thinking-in-Bets at Test Time Reduces Sycophantic Revisions in LLM Agents (2610.34654)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 2026-10-26, cs.SE, preprint only). Source: raw/ft/2610.34654.txt. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: requirement-level stated confidence recorded before and after user feedback; false reversals (breaking a satisfied requirement after mistaken criticism) and successful corrections (fixing a broken requirement after valid feedback), with satisfaction scored by the benchmark's test suite (Section 3.3, Table 1); Brier error of stated confidence (Section 4.1).
Models and benchmarks: five Gemini models (3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.5 Flash, 3.6 Flash, 3.8 Flash, 4.0 Argon); code on SWE-Together, SWE-Interact, and DeepSWE (SWE-Pushback-Hidden, 110 tasks, where a hidden test decides whether a complaint is valid); also report, finance, HLE, and Terminal-Bench (Section 4.1).
Findings: after "a complaint without a failing test case", the standard agent's confidence "drops by roughly the same amount on satisfied and unsatisfied requirements" (Section 4.2, Figure 3a; 10,323 requirement checks, 634 coding turns); satisfied requirements flipped 12.2% (744/6,074) standard vs 0.02% (1/6,130) with the method; requirement-level confidence has lower Brier than whole-patch confidence (Section 4.5).
Leaves open: the feedback is a natural-language complaint, and mistaken complaints carry no failing case (Section 4.1, Section 5 limitations); no executed failing test is shown; no spurious (wrong-oracle) test; whether the size of the confidence update predicts repair success is not reported; closed models, no hidden states.
Breadth:
- confidence families: verbal numeric (requirement and patch level)
- model families: 1 (Gemini, 5 versions)
- benchmarks: SWE-Together, SWE-Interact, DeepSWE, plus non-code
- feedback type: mistaken complaint, valid bug report, hidden-test-graded review complaint
- label source: benchmark test suites and hidden tests
- calibration metrics: Brier; no AUROC
- granularity: requirement and patch
Bears on: L3 Q2/Q7: NARROWS (CLEAN (provisional) to NARROW). L2: no effect.
