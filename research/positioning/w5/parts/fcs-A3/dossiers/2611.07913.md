# Same answer, different future: matched predictions, unmatched updating (2611.07913)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 2026-11-05, preprint only). Cites seed 2607.12985 (Resist-and-Update). Source: raw/ft_2611.07913.html. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: whether states with the same current prediction, prepared by four textual routes (shown record, count summary, told rate, instruction) and two activation tests (steering, hidden-state transplant), update alike under identical new evidence; "exchange rate" weight from regression slopes (Sections 2-3).
Models and benchmarks: Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Gemma-3-12B-it; synthetic Bernoulli coin task with an analytic Bayesian reference (Section 3).
Findings: stated rates move the explicit estimate (slope at least 0.87) and not the document continuation (at most 0.13) (Section 4). A summary stating 8 observations is worth 21 to 71, one stating 64 is worth 52 to 72 (Section 5). Switching the elicitation question changes told-rate weights from "no revision, 288 and 205" to "54, 10 and 23" (Section 5). One-shot steering keeps at most 0.12 of its content after sixteen observations, against 0.42 for the record (Section 6).
Leaves open: no code, no correctness labels, no confidence about own output, no failing-test evidence, no calibration metric, no probe-versus-verbal comparison on correctness.
Breadth:
- confidence families: next-token continuation and expected-count report; no verbal confidence about own correctness
- model families: 3
- scales: 8B to 12B
- benchmarks: synthetic Bernoulli only
- base/instruct pairs: not present
- reasoning vs non-reasoning: Qwen thinking block at single points (Section 4)
- elicitation formats: four report queries (Appendix I)
- label source: analytic Bayesian reference, not execution
Bears on: L3 Q2 and Q7 (update after a shown failing test): no effect, stays CLEAN (provisional); design caution only, since evidence format and elicitation question can move measured update weight by orders of magnitude, so the add-on should fix and vary the query. L2: no effect.
