# MARGIN: runtime confidence calibration for multi-agent coordination (2605.22949)
Status: concurrent (arXiv v1 2026-05-21, v4 2026-10-08; preprint only; one author). Source: raw/ft/2605.22949.pdf (v4), abstract page raw/ft/2605.22949.abs.html. Text basis: FULL TEXT.
Measured: online recalibration of stated (verbalized, 0 to 100) confidence by MARGIN and five online baselines, scored by 10-bin equal-width ECE after workload transitions (Sections 6.1 to 6.3, 7.1).
Models and benchmarks: 18-model pool (9 cloud, 9 local; Qwen, DeepSeek, GPT-OSS, MiniMax, GLM, LLaMA, Phi, Mistral); HumanEval, MBPP, BigCodeBench-Hard, MMLU to MMLU-Pro, GSM8K to MATH. "HumanEval+ and MBPP+ use expanded tests from EvalPlus on the original candidate programs. These two hardening checks change the correctness criterion for previously seen tasks" (Section 6.1, Table 1: HE to HE+ 164 to 163 tasks; MBPP to MBPP+ 257 to 224).
Findings: post-transition raw ECE 8.57 (HE to HE+) and 17.96 (MBPP to MBPP+), MARGIN 3.57 and 3.07 (Table 3); "All five comparisons on each of the two same-task hardening checks are inconclusive" (Section 7.1).
Leaves open: no matched original-tests versus EvalPlus-tests contrast of raw calibration or AUROC on the same cohort; one confidence family (verbal); ranking of confidence methods under the oracle swap not tested; no probe, token, or sampling readout; no release split (LiveCodeBench archived runs excluded, Section 6.1).
Breadth:
- confidence families: verbal stated confidence only
- model families: 8 or more (Section 6.1)
- benchmarks: HE, MBPP, BCB-Hard, HE+, MBPP+ (code)
- base and instruct pairs: not present
- label source: execution on pinned original tests, then EvalPlus tests on the same programs
- label independence: not tested
- contamination split: not present
- calibration metrics: ECE (10 equal-width bins), selection accuracy, pairwise resolution
- granularity: response
Bears on: L1 Q10 (augmented-test oracle swap with calibration measured): NARROWS. L1 Q5, Q13: no effect.
