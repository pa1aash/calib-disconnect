# Ribeiro et al., internal representation of code correctness (2512.07404)
Status: published (ICSE 2026, pp. 1160-1172, Crossref DOI 10.1145/3744916.3787846). Text basis: FULL TEXT, arXiv v3 (2026-01-21, latest; comment "Accepted for ICSE'26"), raw/2512.07404v3.pdf.

Measured: an LAT direction (PCA on hidden-state differences of correct/incorrect pairs, sign and layer set from labels) vs length-normalized likelihood and two reflective readouts, which are token probabilities of verbal answers, not sampled text: Regular "weighted by the model's joint probability", T/F "scored according to the model's assigned probability to True" (Section 2.3).
Models and benchmarks: Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct, OpenCoder-8B-Instruct, CodeLlama-7B-Instruct (4.1.4); HumanEval 151 tasks, BigCodeBench 457 (4.1.1); test splits 121 and 367 tasks; Fit 15 or 46 tasks (Table 2).
Findings: "On BigCodeBench, it improves accuracy by +13.3 to +29.0 pp over the best reflective accuracy; on HumanEval, gains range from +8.7 to +26.2 pp over the strongest baseline" (4.2.1). The RQ1 box and 4.2.3 say +28.8 on BCB (against Random 27.5%). The +26.2 HumanEval gain is Qwen FitSyn 63.0% over Intrinsic 36.8% (4.2.3, Table 4). Mistral HumanEval Regular 40.6% vs T/F 15.3% (4.2.1).
Leaves open: pooled discrimination and calibration metrics, sampling agreement, trained stated confidence, input-only baselines.

Breadth:
- confidence families: likelihood, verbal-category probability (2 formats), LAT; RankEF ranker in RQ2 (5.1.3)
- model families: four instruct; scales 7-8B
- benchmarks: HumanEval, BigCodeBench; MBPP+ and Synthetic for fitting only
- base/instruct, post-training stages, reasoning: not present
- label source: reference unit tests
- label independence: RQ1 correct = reference solution, incorrect = other models' failures (4.1.1); RQ2 self-generated, 10 at T=1 (5.1.1)
- contamination: not present (searched: contamin, cutoff)
- calibration metrics: none; selection accuracy, pass@rank-k
- inference: GEE logistic regression, Benjamini-Hochberg (4.2.1); 10-fold CV mean ± sd (4.1.2)
- granularity: whole solution; all metrics within task

Bears on: L2 (probe vs verbal, code): OCCUPIES, within-task only. Other loci: no effect.
