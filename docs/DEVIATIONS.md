# Deviations from the frozen design

The frozen design is `docs/s7/PIPELINE_DESIGN_FROZEN.md`; its SHA-256 is in `docs/s7/FROZEN_SHA256.txt`, and the pre-commit hook rejects any change to either file. Every departure from the frozen design, however small, is recorded here before or at the time it takes effect, never retroactively edited, and cited in the manuscript where it bears on a reported number.

Each entry has: an ID (DEV-NNN), the date, the section of the frozen design it departs from, what changed, the reason, the impact on the estimands and decisions (none, secondary analyses only, or primary), and whether any study data had been seen when the change was decided.

Pre-registered adjustments that the frozen design itself specifies (the $k$ rule of section 13 and the cuts of K7) are not deviations. Their application is still logged here, with the measured trigger value, so the record is complete.
