## Note for the orchestrator: the row 17 boundary

Ribeiro row 17 is the cell closest to P. Ribeiro re-trains another paper's method under that paper's published configuration, scores it on shared generations, and checks it against the published numbers. Read literally, "reproduction of other papers' published configurations on shared generations" would be met. It is coded Pa because the budget's row 17 is a reconciliation entry: it places other papers' confidence-readout configurations (Ribeiro, Campos, 2610.31820) as points in the budget, to explain why their published conclusions differ. Ribeiro reproduces a ranker baseline and does not reconcile any conclusion. If the paper keeps the literal row text, the row should be reworded (for example, "reproduction of other papers' published readout comparisons on shared generations, as reconciliation points") so that the claim does not rest on a boundary judgment.
